# Peedika

**Launch your local business online in minutes.**

## Vision

Peedika enables local retailers and small businesses to create their own professional online storefront with a custom domain, online payments, and WhatsApp ordering—without requiring technical knowledge or expensive e-commerce platforms.

Unlike Shopify, Peedika focuses on simplicity. Merchants only need a digital storefront to showcase products, collect orders, receive payments, and communicate with customers.
